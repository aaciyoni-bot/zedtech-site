Frequently Asked Questions

Are the products genuine and new?

Yes. Every device we ship is brand new, factory-sealed and unlocked, sourced from top-rated international electronics suppliers — the same factories that supply shops across Africa.

Do I need a credit card or bank account?

No! That's the whole point of ZedTech. All you need is a Mobile Money wallet — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Zamtel Kwacha — with enough balance for your order.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 12–25 days, depending on the item and your location in Zambia. We email you tracking updates along the way.

Do the phones work with Zambian networks?

Yes. Every phone is network-unlocked and works with MTN, Airtel and Zamtel SIM cards out of the box. Each listing shows the specs — RAM, storage, battery and bands — so you know exactly what you're getting before you pay.

Is the price I see the final price?

The price shown includes our service and delivery handling. At checkout a small service fee (5%) is added to cover mobile money charges — the total you see at checkout is exactly what you pay.

What if my order arrives faulty or never comes?

You're covered by our money-back guarantee: if your order doesn't arrive, or arrives faulty, we refund you in full to your mobile money wallet. Just email us at office@orizisgroup.com with your order number.

How do I talk to a real person?

Email us at office@orizisgroup.com — our support team replies 7 days a week, 08:00–20:00. Keep your order number handy so we can help faster.

Need help choosing?

Orders, tracking, refunds or just choosing the right device — email our team at office@orizisgroup.com, 7 days a week, 08:00–20:00.
